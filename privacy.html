Bidoro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Bidoro ("the app", "we") is a personal focus-timer and calendar app. This policy explains what data the app handles, with emphasis on the optional Google Calendar connection.

1. Information we handle

  • Your app data (tasks, focus sessions, calendar events, settings) — stored on your device and, if you sign in, synced to our backend (Supabase) so it is available across your devices.
  • Google Calendar data (optional) — only if you tap "Google Calendar" and sign in. We request the calendar.events scope to read your calendar events and to create/update events you make in Bidoro, so the two calendars stay in sync.
  • Authentication tokens — when you connect Google, we store the OAuth access/refresh tokens for your account on our backend so the sync can run. We never see or store your Google password.

2. How we use Google Calendar data

  • To show your Google Calendar events inside Bidoro, and to push events you create in Bidoro to your Google Calendar (two-way sync).
  • That is the only use. We do not use Google Calendar data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose, and we do not sell it.

3. Limited Use disclosure

Bidoro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the sync feature, to comply with law, or as part of a merger; and we do not use it for ads or to train generalized AI models.

4. Storage & sharing

  • Data is stored on your device and on our backend provider (Supabase). OAuth tokens are kept server-side and are not exposed to other users.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

5. Disconnecting & deleting your data

  • You can revoke Bidoro's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party access. This stops all calendar sync.
  • To delete the calendar tokens and synced data we hold, contact us (below) and we will remove them.
